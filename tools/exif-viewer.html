Free EXIF Metadata Viewer & Remover — See What Your Photos Reveal

Every photo you take with a smartphone or digital camera contains hidden metadata — GPS coordinates, camera model, serial number, date, and settings. This tool reads all that data and lets you download a clean copy with everything stripped. No uploads, no servers, all in your browser.

When to use: Checking photos before sharing online, removing GPS location from images, verifying privacy before sending photos to strangers, auditing what metadata your camera embeds.
